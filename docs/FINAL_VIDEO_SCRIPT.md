## Pronunciation and delivery guide

- **Agla Kadam** = *UG-laa KUH-dum* (अगला कदम). Say it the same way every time; pause slightly after it.
- **Prayagraj** = *pruh-YAAG-raaj*. **Darbhanga** = *dur-BHUN-gaa*.
- **TDR** = "T-D-R" (letter by letter). The first time, you can add "a refund claim".
- **"if approved"** — always say it after an amount. Never say "you will get".
- **"700 crore"** — say "seven hundred crore". Don't convert it to billions.
- **Pace:** about 130 words a minute. Pause for one breath after the opening question and after "So I built Agla Kadam."
- **Energy:** start curious (a real night-time problem), become confident at the demo, and stay calm and honest on slide 6.
- **Hands and eyes:** look at the lens on the face shots; don't read from the screen.
- **Hindi option:** you may say slide 2's last line in Hindi too: "Yatri ko sirf update nahi, agla kadam chahiye."
