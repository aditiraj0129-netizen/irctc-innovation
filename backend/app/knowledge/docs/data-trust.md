Agla Kadam is a prototype. All train running status, timetables and journeys in it are simulated, and trains have 90xxx numbers.
It has no live connection to IRCTC or NTES and never books, cancels or files anything for you.
Refund amounts come from a rules engine that cites each rule, its version and its source. Amounts are estimates, not guarantees.
A TDR refund is decided by the Railway after you file it. Rules that are only announced are labelled as not yet confirmed.
Seat availability on alternative trains is never checked. Always confirm on RailOne, NTES or IRCTC before acting.
