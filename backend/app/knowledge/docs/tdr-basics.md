A TDR (Ticket Deposit Receipt) is the request a passenger files online on IRCTC to claim a refund when they could not travel for a reason such as the train running very late.
Filing a TDR does not mean the money is guaranteed. The Railway checks the claim and decides; the refund is paid only if it is approved.
Keep the TDR reference number and track its status on IRCTC. The money goes back to the original payment method.
