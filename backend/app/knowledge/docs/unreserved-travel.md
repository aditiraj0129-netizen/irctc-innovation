An unreserved (General) ticket lets you travel only in the General (unreserved) coaches of a train that has them.
A reserved ticket for one train is not a ticket for a different train, so if you switch to another train you need a new ticket for it.
Unreserved tickets can be bought at the station counter or on the official unreserved ticketing apps (UTS or RailOne) before you board.
Passengers travelling irregularly in reserved coaches are liable to be charged a penalty and excess fare.
Some trains, such as chair-car-only trains, have no General coaches, and some local trains are unreserved only.
Check the coach composition of the train before relying on unreserved travel. Seats in General coaches are not guaranteed.
