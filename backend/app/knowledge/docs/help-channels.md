For railway enquiries, passengers can call 139. Complaints and help requests can be made on RailMadad.
For security on trains and stations, the Railway Protection Force helpline is 182.
For online fraud, the national cyber crime helpline is 1930. In any emergency, call 112.
Never share an OTP or payment PIN with anyone who calls claiming to be from the railways.
