Tatkal booking opens one day before the journey date, not counting the day of travel.
For AC classes Tatkal booking opens at 10:00, and for non-AC classes it opens at 11:00.
Online Tatkal booking needs Aadhaar-based OTP authentication on the IRCTC account.
Authorised agents cannot book Tatkal tickets in the first 30 minutes of the window.
A Tatkal ticket is never guaranteed: it depends on availability at the time of booking, and a confirmed Tatkal ticket normally gets no refund on cancellation.
